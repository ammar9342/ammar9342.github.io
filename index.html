❓ What Is This?

qcom-diag-lisa is a Magisk module that enables the Qualcomm diagnostic (diag) interface on your Xiaomi 11 Lite 5G NE (codenamed "lisa") when running custom ROMs like HyperOS or LineageOS.

Think of it as a special key that unlocks a hidden communication channel on your phone's Qualcomm chipset. This is useful for developers, network engineers, or tech enthusiasts who want to access advanced device diagnostics, read logs, or test radio functionality.

✨ Benefits

  • 🔧 Enable Diag Interface - Activate the hidden Qualcomm diagnostic port
  • 📶 Better Network Logging - Capture detailed radio and signal data
  • 🔄 Works on Custom ROMs - Specifically designed for HyperOS and LineageOS builds
  • 📦 Easy Installation - Install via Magisk app with just a couple of taps
  • 🔍 Developer Friendly - Ideal for QXDM, QPST, and similar diagnostic tools
  • ⚡ Lightweight - Minimal footprint, no performance impact
  • 🛠️ Specifically for Xiaomi 11 Lite 5G NE - Tailored for your device's architecture

📋 What You Need

  • 📱 Xiaomi 11 Lite 5G NE with an unlocked bootloader
  • 🔓 Magisk installed on your device (version 24 or later recommended)
  • 🖥️ A Windows computer for downloading and transferring the file
  • 📦 USB cable to connect your phone to your computer

Step 2: Transfer the File

Once downloaded, use your USB cable to connect your Xiaomi phone to your Windows computer. Copy the downloaded module ZIP file to any folder on your phone's internal storage (for example, the Downloads folder).

Step 3: Open Magisk App

On your phone, open the Magisk application. Make sure you have root access (the app should show a green checkmark and "Magisk installed" message on its main screen).

Step 4: Install the Module

In Magisk app, tap the 🧩 Modules icon at the bottom (it looks like a puzzle piece). Then tap "Install from storage" button. Navigate to the folder where you placed the ZIP file and select it. Magisk will install the module automatically.

Step 5: Reboot Your Phone

After installation completes, tap "Reboot" when prompted. Your phone will restart. This usually takes about 30-60 seconds.

Step 6: Verify It Works

After reboot, your Qualcomm diag interface is now enabled. You can verify this by connecting your phone to a Windows PC and checking Device Manager - you should see a new "Qualcomm HS-USB Diagnostics" device appear in the Ports section. You can now use tools like QPST or QXDM to connect to your phone.

🔧 Troubleshooting

  • Diag not showing up? - Make sure your device is in "charging" USB mode, not "file transfer" mode. Change USB settings in your phone's notification panel.
  • Magisk module not appearing? - Confirm you have Magisk properly installed. Open Magisk app and check "Ramdisk" section shows "Yes".
  • Boot loop after install? - Don't panic! Reboot to recovery (hold Power + Volume Down) and uninstall the module from Magisk recovery mode.
  • Not working on your ROM? - This module is optimized for HyperOS and LineageOS. Other AOSP-based ROMs may have compatibility issues.
  • Still having issues? - Visit the GitHub issues page for help and community support.

❔ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe for my phone?

Yes. The module simply activates a software port that already exists on your Qualcomm chipset. It doesn't modify system files or break warranty (since you're already on a custom ROM with unlocked bootloader).

Will this drain my battery?

No noticeable impact. The diag interface remains idle unless actively being used by diagnostic software.

Can I remove it later?

Absolutely. Open Magisk app, go to Modules, find qcom-diag-lisa, and tap "Remove". Reboot to complete uninstallation.

Do I need a computer to use this after installation?

Yes. The primary purpose is to enable diag mode for communication with Windows-based tools. The module itself is installed on phone, but you'll need Windows for actual diag work.
